Fair questions.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, with every feature included. Free keeps 3 projects at a time and includes 10 exports a month. Free exports carry a small ReelForge watermark; paid plans remove it.

How long can my videos be?

Up to three minutes on Free and Basic, five on Pro and ten on Advance.

Do I need to know video editing?

No. If you can edit a document, you can edit in ReelForge.

Which languages do captions support?

English (US, UK and India), Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German.

Can I use my own B-roll?

Yes. Upload your own clips, or search the built-in stock library.
